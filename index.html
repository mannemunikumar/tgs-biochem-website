About Our Lab & Team

TGS Biochem Analytical Labs combines analytical laboratory instrumentation with computational structural biology and molecular modeling to provide high-precision validation across agrochemical, food, and environmental industries.

Food Biochemistry & Nutritional Sciences

Dr. Giridhar Goudar

Biochemist & Analytical Research Lead

  • • Nutritional Profiling & Bioactives: Specializes in biochemical characterization of polyphenols, flavonoids, and micronutrients in plant matrices and functional foods.
  • • By-Product Valorization: Focuses on the extraction, recovery, and stability of biologically active compounds from agro/food waste streams.
  • • In Vitro Digestion Models: Simulates gastrointestinal digestion to assess bioavailability, stability, and antioxidant potency of dietary compounds.
  • • Lipid Metabolism & Biomarkers: Evaluates functional food interventions on lipid parameters (HDL, LDL, VLDL) and metabolic markers like Gb3.
  • • Analytical Food Safety: Applies standard spectrophotometric and chromatographic assays to verify nutrient density and authenticity.

Bioinformatics & Molecular Modeling

Dr. Manne Munikumar

Bioinformatics & Computational Biology Lead

  • • Structural Biology & Modeling: Specializes in in silico target identification, 3D macromolecular structure prediction (homology modeling), and structural refinement.
  • • Docking & Virtual Screening: Employs high-throughput screening and docking simulations to evaluate ligand-protein binding mechanisms and affinities.
  • • Molecular Dynamics (MD): Analyzes thermodynamic stability, conformational trajectories, and root-mean-square metrics (RMSD/RMSF) of biological complexes.
  • • NGS & Genomics: Conducts high-throughput genomic data processing, variant calling, and transcriptomic data interpretation.
  • • In Silico ADMET & Vaccines: Evaluates pharmacokinetic profiles, drug-likeness, toxicity risks, and multi-epitope vaccine construct design.

Our Guiding Principles

Vision & Mission

Driving analytical excellence through rigorous biochemical assays, standard compliance, and computational innovation.

Our Vision

To become a benchmark laboratory and global research partner in analytical testing, food safety, water security, and computational bio-solutions, enabling sustainable agricultural and environmental ecosystems worldwide.

Our Mission

To provide accurate, reproducible, and rapid analytical services for food, water, and soil samples using state-of-the-art chromatographic, spectroscopic, and computational tools while upholding strict regulatory compliance.

Analytical Capabilities

Comprehensive Testing Services

High-precision laboratory testing protocols, biochemical diagnostics, and research computational analysis for Food, Water, and Soil.

Food Analysis

Standardized nutritional, chemical, and microbiological profiling for regulatory compliance and consumer safety.

  • Nutritional Profiling (Proximate Analysis: Protein, Fat, Carbohydrates, Energy)
  • Pesticide Residue & Chemical Contaminant Screening
  • Food Adulteration & Authenticity Verification
  • Microbial Pathogen Testing (E. coli, Salmonella, Yeast & Molds)
  • Preservative, Additive & Food Color Quantification
  • Shelf-Life & Accelerated Stability Studies

Water Testing

Complete physicochemical, toxicological, and microbial assays for drinking, borewell, and industrial water.

  • Potable & Packaged Drinking Water Testing (IS 10500 / IS 14543)
  • Physicochemical Analysis (pH, TDS, Turbidity, Total Hardness, Alkalinity)
  • Trace Heavy Metal Detection (Lead, Arsenic, Cadmium, Chromium, Mercury)
  • Microbiological Testing (Coliforms, E. coli, Faecal Streptococci)
  • Effluent & Wastewater Quality Profiling (BOD, COD, TSS, Oil & Grease)
  • Agricultural & Irrigation Water Suitability Evaluation
Request Water Testing →

Soil & Agro Testing

Fertility mapping, mineral balance, and environmental remediation tests for agriculture and environmental projects.

  • Soil Fertility Assessment & Primary Nutrients (NPK Ratio)
  • Secondary Nutrients & Micronutrients (Ca, Mg, S, Zn, Fe, Cu, Mn, B)
  • Soil Physical Properties (pH, Electrical Conductivity, Texture, Bulk Density)
  • Organic Matter & Total Organic Carbon (TOC)
  • Heavy Metals & Soil Contaminant Remediation Analysis
  • Soil Biological Activity & Microbial Population Counts
